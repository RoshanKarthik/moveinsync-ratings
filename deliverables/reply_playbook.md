# Reply playbook — Play Store reviews

For ticket **MIS-01** in `deliverables/recommendation.html`. Generated by `uv run ratings recommend`
from `backlog.yaml` and Play Store reviews up to 11 Oct 2026.

## The rules (Google Play)

- **Address the user's specific comment.** Name the problem they described; no generic thank-you on a 1★ review.
- **Be respectful**, even when the review is not.
- **Never solicit.** Do not ask the user to change, raise or remove their rating, and do not offer anything in return.
  Google notifies the reviewer of a reply by push and email; they decide whether to update.
- **No personal data in public.** Move the conversation to email (`app@moveinsync.com`, the address used in earlier
  replies) or the in-app Help section; ask for the trip date and work email there, never in the reply.
- **Promise only what is scheduled.** Name a release only once it is on Google Play.

Sources: `docs/SOURCES.md`.

## Working the queue

1. **Today:** the unanswered 1–2★ reviews from the last 90 days
   (203 of 205), most “found helpful” first — Play shows those first.
2. **Every working day:** new 1–2★ reviews within 2 working days; 3★ weekly.
3. **Safety concerns:** same day, and escalate to Safety & compliance before replying.
4. **Route** using the issue tags in the monitor (each review's issue is already tagged), then reply.
5. **When a fix ships:** post the “after the fix” reply on reviews that named the issue.

### Crash, blank screen or won't open · Slow to load / hangs · 'Not downloaded from Play Store' error

- **Route to:** Mobile engineering (attach app version and device from the review)
- **Unanswered 1–2★ reviews naming these, last 90 days:** 47

> Sorry the app {crashed / would not open / was slow} for you, especially with your cab on the way. Our engineers are working on this. Please update to the latest version; if it still happens, write to app@moveinsync.com with your phone model so we can check it directly.

*After the fix ships:*

> An update that fixes this problem is now on Google Play (version {x}). Thank you for reporting it.

### Login OTP not received · Frequent logout / re-login · Onboarding / registration confusion

- **Route to:** Platform / backend (OTP) · Product (sessions)
- **Unanswered 1–2★ reviews naming these, last 90 days:** 15

> Sorry you could not sign in. If the OTP does not arrive within a minute, please use the resend option; if it still does not arrive, write to app@moveinsync.com from your work email with your mobile operator, and we will check delivery for your number.

*After the fix ships:*

> We have added a second way to receive the OTP and you will be asked to sign in less often in version {x}. Thank you for telling us.

## Do not

- “Please update your rating” / “We hope you'll give us 5 stars” — soliciting, against Google Play policy.
- Copy-paste the same text on every review — readers notice, and it does not address the comment.
- Argue with the reviewer or blame the employer, the driver or the user in public.
- Reply with a generic thank-you to a complaint.
